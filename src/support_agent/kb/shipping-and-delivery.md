# Shipping & Delivery

## Delivery times
Standard shipping within the US takes 3-7 business days after the order ships. Orders usually ship within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping costs $5.00 and is free on orders over $75.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom and most of the European Union. International orders take 7-14 business days and cost a flat $15.00. Import duties and taxes may be charged by the destination country and are the customer's responsibility.

## Tracking your order
Once your order ships you receive a tracking number by email. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan. If your package is in transit and within its estimated delivery date, it is on its way - no action is needed.

## Changing an address or cancelling
You can change the shipping address or cancel an order only while it is still processing (before it ships). Once an order has shipped we cannot change the address or cancel it; you can refuse delivery or return it after it arrives.

## Lost packages
If the carrier marks a package as lost, or tracking has shown no movement for 7 or more days, we send a free replacement or issue a full refund - your choice.

## Marked delivered but not received
Sometimes carriers mark a package delivered a day early. Please wait 48 hours, check with neighbours and around your property. If it still hasn't turned up, our team opens an investigation with the carrier and follows up with you.
