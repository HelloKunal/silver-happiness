# Account & Billing

## Password reset and login problems
If you can't log in, we can send a password reset link to the email address on your account. For your security we never send reset links to a different address and never ask for your password.

## Changing your account email
Email address changes are verified by our team before they take effect, to protect your account from takeover. You will receive a confirmation at both the old and new address.

## Charged twice?
When you place an order your bank may show a temporary authorization in addition to the final charge. Pending authorizations disappear on their own within 3-5 business days. If you were genuinely charged twice for the same order (two completed charges), we refund the duplicate charge.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery.

## Price adjustments and price matching
We do not offer price matching or retroactive price adjustments if an item goes on sale after you buy it.

## Membership tiers
Standard: free shipping on orders over $75. Silver: free shipping on orders over $50 and early access to sales. Gold: free shipping on every order, early access to sales and a dedicated support queue. Tiers are based on the last 12 months of purchases.
