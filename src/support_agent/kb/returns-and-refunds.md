# Returns & Refunds

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Refunds go back to the original payment method and typically appear within 5-10 business days after we issue them. Requests after 30 days are treated as exceptions and reviewed by our team case by case.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, we will make it right at no cost to you: either a full refund of the affected item's price plus the original shipping charge, or a free replacement shipped to you. Customers do not need to return damaged goods unless we ask. A photo helps but is not required.

## Final sale items
Items marked "final sale" (for example clearance goods) cannot be returned or refunded for change-of-mind reasons. Final sale items that arrive damaged or defective are still covered by our damaged-item policy.

## Partial refunds
If only one item in a multi-item order has a problem, we refund that item only. Other items in the order are unaffected.

## Refund already issued
Each order can be refunded only up to the amount that was paid. If a refund has already been issued, you can see it on the order; banks can take up to 10 business days to show it.

## Store credit
In some cases we can offer store credit instead of a refund. Store credit never expires.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are non-refundable and never expire.
